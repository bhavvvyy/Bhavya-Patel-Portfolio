HOW TO PUT THIS SITE ONLINE (free, about 5 minutes)

1. Open index.html in a text editor and make these edits:
   - Search for  your-linkedin-id  and replace it with your LinkedIn profile name.
   - Search for  https://YOUR-SITE.vercel.app  and replace it with your real site address
     (do this after step 2 gives you the address, then upload again). LinkedIn needs it to show the preview image.
2. Go to vercel.com, sign in with GitHub, choose Add New > Project, and upload this folder
   (or push it to a GitHub repo and import that repo). No build settings are needed.
3. Paste the final address into your LinkedIn profile under Contact info > Website, and into the Featured section.
4. To refresh the LinkedIn preview after changes, open https://www.linkedin.com/post-inspector/ and inspect your address.
